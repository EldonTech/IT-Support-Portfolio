## About Me

Hi, I'm Eldon Jannamon! I'm a recent Information Technology graduate from Full Sail University, and I'm passionate about IT Support, solving technical problems, and helping others when they run into issues.

My journey into IT started when I helped a friend fix a network connection issue. After we got it working, my friend suggested I consider a career in IT. That conversation stuck with me, and it inspired me to pursue a career in technology.

Since then, I've completed a Certificate in Information Technology at DeVry University, where I earned my CompTIA IT Fundamentals (ITF+). I then earned my CompTIA A+ certification through self-study. Most recently, I graduated from Full Sail University with my associate degree in Information Technology. I'm currently working toward my CompTIA Network+ and Security+ certifications to continue expanding my knowledge.

Outside of studying, I'm building home labs to gain hands-on experience with Windows, Linux, networking, and troubleshooting. I want to understand not only how technology works, but also how to identify problems and find solutions when things don't go as planned.

My goal is to land an opportunity in IT Help Desk or IT Support where I can apply what I've learned, help others solve technical problems, and continue growing my skills through real-world experience. I'm excited to keep learning, earn future certifications, and build a career in an industry I'm passionate about.
