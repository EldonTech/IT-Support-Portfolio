## 👋 About Me

Hey, I'm Eldon! I'm a recent Information Technology graduate from Full Sail University, working toward starting my career in IT Support.

My interest in IT started when I helped a friend fix a network connection issue. After we got it working, my friend suggested I consider a career in IT. That conversation stuck with me, and it helped inspire me to pursue IT seriously.

Since then, I've earned my CompTIA ITF+ and A+ certifications and completed my associate degree in Information Technology. I completed my ITF+ studies through DeVry University and prepared for my A+ certification through self-study. I'm now working toward CompTIA Network+ with support from Full Sail University.

Outside of studying, I'm building home labs to get hands-on experience with Windows, networking, and troubleshooting. I want to understand not just how technology works, but also how to solve problems when things don't go as planned.

My goal is to land my first opportunity in IT Help Desk or IT Support, continue learning from real-world experience, and grow my skills one project at a time.

Thanks for stopping by! I'm excited to share what I build and learn along the way.
